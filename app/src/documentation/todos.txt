What you need to do for Google Play
Before you publish to Google Play with ads, you must:
1.Create an AdMob Account: You need to register your app there to get a unique "Ad Unit ID."
2.Declare Ads: In the Google Play Console, you must check a box stating "My app contains ads." This adds the "Contains Ads" label to your store listing.
3.Privacy Policy: Because ads track user data (like device IDs), you are legally required to have a Privacy Policy link in your app and on your store listing.


------------average calculation
PDC match averages are calculated as a three-dart average using total points scored divided by total darts thrown, multiplied by 3.
3-Dart Average = (Total Points Scored/Total Darts Thrown) * 3
Cumulative across the match: The statistic is not an average of individual leg scores. Instead, every point scored in every leg is summed up and divided by the entire match dart count.
Losing legs count: For a leg a player does not win, only the actual points scored (e.g., (501 - score remaining) and the actual darts thrown by that player in that leg are added to the totals.
Winning turn rule: If a player hits a checkout on the 1st or 2nd dart of a visit, only 1 or 2 darts are added to the total dart count for that visit (not a full 3).
Bust rule: If a player busts their score on the 1st or 2nd dart, the visit is treated as a full 3 darts thrown with 0 points scored for that turn.
